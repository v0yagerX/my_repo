## 1. Thesis

1. **The early-October move was a failed breakout, not a retest.**
   - SOXX broke above its Aug–Sep range (closes 497.40–559.12, intraday top 566.02) on Sep 21–22. It then cleared the late-September shelf at 572.78 on Oct 1–2.
   - It peaked at 589.51 (close) / 596.44 (intraday). That is a *lower high* than the Jun 22 peak of 655.01.
   - It has since given the whole move back. The Oct 14 close of 519.42 is below the range midpoint (528.26), and the overnight print of 510.16 is below the Sep 17 launch gap (510.37–514.80).
2. **Projected bottoming zone: SOXX 465–490 on a closing basis (central estimate ~478).**

   | Measured from | Drawdown |
   |---|---|
   | Oct 14 close (519.42) | **−5.7% to −10.5%** |
   | Oct 5 closing high (589.51) | −16.9% to −21.1% |
   | Jun 22 record close (655.01) | −25.2% to −29.0% |

   - Equivalent zones: SMH ≈ 504–532, SOX ≈ 10,450–11,040.
3. **Timing: the low most likely forms Oct 28 – Nov 20 (10–25 sessions out), not this week.**
   - The base-rate median trough date is **Nov 4**.
   - I put **~55%** on SOXX closing at or below 493 before it closes at or above 559.
   - I put **~30%** on a close below the Jul 29 low of 465. That would be a trend change, opening **435–455 into December**.

## 4. Base rates and analogs

### Supporting

**1. SOX since 1995: the first close ≥11% below its 60-session closing high.** Today the SOX is −11.6% and SOXX −11.9% [calc]. This count is unbiased: it includes signals that bottomed on the day.

| Sample | n | Median further decline | Further ≥5% | Further ≥10% | Further ≥20% | Median sessions to trough |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1995–2026 | 57 | **−5.7%** | 51% | 42% | 32% | **15** |
| Since 2016 | 20 | −6.0% | 55% | 35% | 20% | 14.5 |

Applied to SOXX, the 1995–2026 median puts the trough at about **490 around Nov 4**.

**2. Lower-high failure subset (n=14).** The setup: a 1-year high, a drop of at least 20%, a rally of at least 15% that fails at least 8% below the 1-year high, then a pullback of at least 11%.
- The outcomes are **bimodal**.
- 5 of 14 (36%) undercut the prior trough (1996, 1998, 2001, 2002, 2008), with further declines of 24–62%.
- 8 of the other 9 bottomed within 0–17 sessions, with at most 4.6% further decline. The exception, Dec 2002, fell another 22% without undercutting the prior trough.
- The sample is small and weighted toward 2001–2009.

**3. The upper layers already imply my zone.**
- SOXX's realized down-leg ratio to QQQ is 2.8–2.9×, and its down-day beta is 2.25 [calc].
- **QQQ only needs to reach 700** (the Sep 16 low and 61.8% Fibonacci, −4.0% from the close) for SOXX to be at about 468–478 at β 2.0–2.5.
- **[MV] shallow path:** QQQ at 695–710 maps to SOXX at about 459–492.
- **[MV] deeper path:** QQQ at 660–685 maps to SOXX at about 400–457.
- **The bull's own regression** puts SOXX at 465 with RSP flat at SPX ≈ 7,619 [market_bull.md], inside [MV]'s shallow zone. My case does not need a market crash.
- My timing (Oct 28 – Nov 10 core) matches [MV]'s window for high-beta lows in the deeper scenario.

### Against (honestly)

- **The DB analog:** SOXX 15–25% below its record, below the 50-day, above a rising 200-day; n=17, since 2021.
  - It shows a **40-day median return of +10.4%, positive 86% of the time** [DB].
  - Its median 40-day maximum adverse excursion is −7.1% (≈482) and its 25th percentile is −9.4% (≈471), so the typical path still visits my zone.
  - Its 200-day filter excludes trend breaks by construction.
- **Quick bottoms are common:** 40% of the 57 signals bottomed within 5 sessions.
- **The base_rates.json SOXX conditionals** count only episodes of 8% or more. [MV] showed that this method biases the conditional statistics deeper, so I do not rely on them.
