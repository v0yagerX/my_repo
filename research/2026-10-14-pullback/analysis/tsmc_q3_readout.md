# TSMC Q3 2026 readout

*Written 06:52 UTC Thu Oct 15, 2026 (2:52 am ET). The earnings call (began 06:00 UTC) was still in Q&A when this was written. IBKR data is read-only quotes; no orders, alerts or watchlists were created. Nothing below is filled in from memory: every number has a source and a time.*

## 0. Bottom line

- **The fundamentals are a beat-and-raise, so they point toward "shallow".**
  - **Q3 beat.** Net profit was NT$763.06bn against the LSEG SmartEstimate of NT$746.3bn (+2.2%). Revenue was US$46.72bn, above the top of the US$44.6–45.8bn guide. Operating margin was 58.7%, above the top of the 56–58% guide. Gross margin was 66.4%, mid-range of the 65–67% guide but a little below the 66.9–67.5% that sell-side analysts had pencilled in.
  - **Q4 guide: up strongly quarter on quarter.** Revenue US$52.3–54.3bn, which is **+11.9% to +16.2% q/q (midpoint +14.1%)** and +58% y/y. Gross margin 65–67%, operating margin 56.5–58.5%, at an FX assumption of 31.8 NT$/US$.
  - **Full-year 2026 growth raised** to "about 44%" in US$, from "slightly above 40%".
  - **2026 capex reaffirmed at US$60–64bn.** Capex for 2026–2028 is expected to be "significantly higher" than in the past three years.
  - **AI commentary:** "very strong", "multi-year structural", "won't leave food on the table". N2-family capacity will more than double in 2027, and a further 3nm fab is being added in Tainan.
- **Every "toward shallow" fundamental box is ticked except two:**
  - I found no explicit CoWoS "demand exceeds supply" quote in the coverage I could reach.
  - The 2027 commentary is mixed. Management said it will be "prudent" in planning 2027 because consumer and price-sensitive end markets face rising component prices and macro uncertainty. It gave no 2027 growth number.
- **None of the fundamental triggers for "deeper" or "trend break" fired:**
  - The guide is up, not flat or down.
  - The gross-margin guide is ≥65%, not below 63–64%.
  - There was no digestion or order-adjustment language.
  - Capex was not cut.
- **The tape has not confirmed. If anything, the early overnight tape is leaning the wrong way.**
  - The TSM ADR rallied on the guide from 420.50 (05:40 UTC) to 427.00 (06:15 UTC), then gave it all back: **420.50 (−2.21%) at 06:50 UTC**.
  - SOXX overnight was **509.30 (−1.95%)** at 06:50 UTC. That is below 512.6, and below the 510 "TSMC beat that closes SOXX red below 510" invalidation in the semis verdict.
  - NVDA overnight was 218.02 (−1.48%), still above 216.7.
  - Behind this: a macro risk-off (hot September CPI, TAIEX −3.41%, KOSPI and KOSDAQ circuit breakers).
- **Probabilities: no formal shift yet.** The verdicts' TSMC rules need both the fundamentals and a holding tape (US closes on Oct 15 and Oct 16, the options-expiration day).
  - Semis stay **25/37/38** and NVDA stays **35/45/20** until the US close on Oct 15.
  - The flat or down-guide route to trend break is now closed. The only route left to ~42% trend break is a **faded beat**, and the overnight tape is flirting with one.

- **Node mix (wafer revenue):** N2 8% (2Q 3%), N3 33%, N5 28%, N7 10%. Advanced nodes (≤7nm) were 79%.
- **Platforms:** HPC was 68% (2Q 66%, 3Q25 57%) and grew **+22% q/q**. Smartphone was 21% (+10% q/q). Automotive grew +23% q/q, IoT +5%, DCE −12%.
- **Geography:** North America 79% (2Q 78%), **China 5%** (2Q 6%, 3Q25 8%).
- **Inventory: no sign of digestion.**
  - Days of inventory fell from 87 to **82**, "primarily due to the increase in wafer shipments".
  - Inventory value rose from NT$385.5bn to NT$427.8bn (+11%), against revenue +17.6%.
  - Wafer shipments were 4,476k 12-inch equivalent (+3.2% q/q).
- **Capex: US$13.84bn in Q3** (2Q 15.70, 1Q 11.10); **US$40.64bn year to date.** Holding the US$60–64bn range implies **US$19.4–23.4bn in Q4, +40% to +69% q/q**. That would be a heavy Q4 (good for semicap orders), but it also means a January actual below US$60bn would read as a de facto trim.
- **Cash:** free cash flow was NT$476.6bn. Cash and marketable securities were NT$4.02tn. Other investing inflows of NT$198.7bn were "mainly attributable to U.S. Treasury Department Investment Tax Credits".
- **Dividend:** the last declared dividend is for Q2: NT$7.00, approved Aug 11, ex-dividend Dec 10, paid Jan 7, 2027. **No Q3 dividend was declared in today's materials.** The CFO restated the commitment to a "sustainable and steadily increasing" cash dividend per share, on both an annual and a quarterly basis (UDN 06:23).

**Full-year 2026:**
- Growth is now "**about 44%**" in US$ (UDN 06:40, 06:42), raised from "slightly above 40%" in July.
- Pre-call street range was 40–46%; Morgan Stanley was at about 46%. So this is a **raise**, but not to the top of the range.

**Capex** (CFO Wendell Huang, UDN 06:23):
- 2026 is **reaffirmed at US$60–64bn**. This is **held, not cut or raised.**
- 2026–2028 capex is expected to be "**significantly higher than the past 3 years**", given "sustained and strong multi-year demand from customers, including the emerging agentic-AI market".
- Tool supply is tight; TSMC works with tool vendors in advance.

**AI and demand language** (Chairman C.C. Wei, UDN 06:40 and 06:42):
- "The AI market continues to be very strong; recent developments are very positive for the AI semiconductor demand outlook."
- AI-related demand is "supported by strong underlying demand for AI accelerators". The rise of gen-AI CPUs and growing networking-chip demand add to silicon demand.
- AI is a "multi-year, structural growth opportunity".
- TSMC sent teams to the US to check data-center construction and power availability. Feedback was good, and cloud providers "are ready". TSMC will "expand capacity at full speed and increase capex".
- Wei does not intend to "leave food on the table for others" (UDN 06:37).
- **CoWoS / advanced packaging:** I found **no explicit "demand exceeds supply" CoWoS quote** in the coverage published by 06:50. This is still open; it may come up in Q&A or appear in the transcript.

**2027:**
- **Positive capacity signals:**
  - N2-family annual capacity will **more than double in 2027**.
  - A14 volume production is due in 2028, with readiness in 2027.
  - Three new 3nm fabs are being added globally (unusual for a mature node), **including another Tainan 3nm fab (volume production 1H28)** "driven by strong customer demand". The existing new Tainan 3nm fab starts 1H27.
  - Arizona fab 2 starts volume production in **3Q27, slightly ahead of plan**. Japan fab 2 will use 3nm, from 2028.
  - Some 5nm tools are being converted to 3nm.
  - TSMC plans 13 fabs in Taiwan and 5–6 overseas over the next few years (UDN 06:31 and 06:37; 財訊 06:38).
- **The hedge (UDN 06:42, close paraphrase of the prepared remarks):** "Consumer and price-sensitive end markets are being challenged by rising component prices and macroeconomic uncertainty. **Therefore, in planning 2027 we will remain prudent.**"
  - The caution is aimed at non-AI and consumer markets, **not** at AI.
  - There was **no 2027 revenue-growth figure**.

**N2 ramp:**
- N2 was 8% of Q3 wafer revenue. It is diluting gross margin in Q3 and Q4. Capacity is ramping in phases at Hsinchu and Kaohsiung.

**Overseas fab dilution:**
- Named as a contributor to the Q4 gross-margin step-down (UDN 06:30).
- One outlet (Yahoo, 06:22) quotes a full-year 2026 overseas dilution of about 2–3pp. **I do not rely on this:** the same article contains several stale or incorrect figures (§6).
- The CFO said TSMC will use its growing scale in Arizona and operational improvements to manage the impact, and expects to remain "the most cost-effective manufacturer" in every region.

**China, export controls, tariffs:**
- **None found** in the coverage accessed by 06:50. China was 5% of revenue.
- Related items:
  - Wei confirmed TSMC has **sent a team to evaluate a Texas fab** and is in capacity-cooperation talks with a customer that plans to build its own fab. No decision has been made (UDN 06:43).
  - Separately, Trump said overnight that he had recently spoken with C.C. Wei (Yahoo/UDN headlines 03:59–04:58 UTC; not verified further).

**Digestion or order-adjustment language:** **none seen.**
