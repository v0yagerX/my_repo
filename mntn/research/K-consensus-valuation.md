### 1b. Which EPS is "consensus"? (GAAP vs adjusted)

**MNTN does not report non-GAAP EPS.** Its releases give GAAP EPS and adjusted EBITDA only [reported, Q2'26 8-K]. Data vendors therefore publish inconsistent EPS series.

| Vendor | Q2'26 EPS "consensus" | Q2'26 EPS "actual" | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Investing.com | $0.15 | $0.09 | GAAP diluted ([2026-08-04](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-mntn-q2-2026-profit-miss-sparks-afterhours-drop-93CH-4835959)) |

| Zacks | $0.22 | "$0.11 adjusted" | Vendor-defined ([2026-08-04](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/mntn-inc-mntn-lags-q2-230014480.html)) |
| Yahoo / S&P Global | $0.20 | $0.18 | "Normalized", which appears to exclude SBC and amortization ([Analysis](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MNTN/analysis/)) |

The FY26 "normalized" $1.05 implies about $83M of net income at ~79M diluted shares [estimate: 1.05 × 79M]. That is roughly (adj. EBITDA $98.5M + interest ~$8M) × (1 − ~22–25% tax). So the series excludes SBC and amortization. **For modeling, treat the $1.05 / $1.31 consensus as an adjusted, SBC-excluded figure, not GAAP.**

### 3a. Why Q2'26 GAAP EPS missed [reported inputs; bridge is my estimate]

Bridge [estimate]:

**So the "miss" was non-cash and below adjusted EBITDA. Revenue and adj. EBITDA were in line or at the upper end of guidance.**

### 3b. Pattern

- **Revenue:**
  - Beat or in line in 5 of 5 quarters.
  - Apart from the +6% IPO-quarter beat, every surprise was within ±1.4%.
  - Dossier B shows the guide is set close to the outcome, about 0–1% cushion.
- **GAAP EPS:**
  - Two clean beats: Q3'25, and Q4'25, which was tax-aided.

- **Adj. EBITDA:** beat the high end of guidance in Q3'25, Q4'25 and Q1'26. Q2'26 landed in the upper half. **The cushion has narrowed.**
- **What moves the stock is the revenue guide vs consensus, not EPS:**
  - FY26 guide above consensus: **+37%**.
  - Q2 guide about 2% below consensus: **−23%**.
  - Q4'25 guide only "bracketing" consensus: **−8%**.
  - EPS miss with a buyback and Q3 acceleration: **+4%**.
- **The Nov 4, 2026 print** therefore hinges on:
  - the Q4 guide vs ~$108M consensus, which requires about 24.5% y/y growth; and
  - the first FY27 framing vs the $417M consensus.

### 5d. What the price implies (reverse-DCF sketch) [estimate]

Method: Gordon growth on EV, where EV = FCF × (1 + g) / (r − g), solved for g.

| Discount rate | FY26E FCF of $74M (midpoint, incl. ~$8M interest) | Unlevered FCF of $66M (ex-interest) | FCF − SBC of ~$45M |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10% | g = −3.5% | g = −2.2% | g = +1.4% |
| 11% | g = −2.6% | g = −1.3% | g = +2.3% |
| 12% | g = −1.8% | g = −0.4% | g = +3.2% |

- **Pre-SBC:** the market prices MNTN as a business whose FCF **shrinks about 0.5–3.5% a year forever**.
- **SBC-burdened:** it prices growth of only **+1–3%**.

- **At the long-term target margin:** 35–40% on a hypothetical FY28 revenue of ~$480M (FY27 consensus +15%) gives EBITDA of $168–192M, i.e. **2.8–3.1x the current EV**.
- **Reading:** the stock is priced for de-rating or disintermediation, not on today's numbers.

## 7. Implications for the thesis (AI victim vs AI winner)

- **The sell side treats MNTN as an AI beneficiary.** Examples: Tigress (AI-enabled self-serve), Douglas ("AI-native"), and QuickFrame AI.
  - Estimates have *risen* since IPO: Evercore's FY26 EBITDA went from $90M to the $96–101M guide.
  - Post-Q2 EPS revisions are up.
- **The market prices it like an AI victim.**
  - ~4.3–4.6x FY27E EBITDA and ~7x EV/FCF.
  - Implied perpetual FCF *decline* of ~0.5–3.5% (pre-SBC).
  - Mean PT $18.10 is +77% above the price, but targets keep falling to chase the price.
- **This topic does not prove either case.** It shows the debate is about **terminal value and durability**: agentic or walled-garden disintermediation of SMB CTV buying. Near-term numbers are not the issue.
- **Asymmetry:**
  - If 2H26 delivers ~24–25% growth and FY27 lands near $417M / ~$120M+ EBITDA, the stock is cheap on any conventional multiple.
  - The bear case needs FY27–28 growth to fall to the low teens *and* margins to stall below 30%.
- **Net:** neutral-to-slightly-positive for "AI winner" on fundamentals so far, but the market's verdict is "victim until proven otherwise." **The Nov 4 Q4 guide and first FY27 commentary are the next test.**
