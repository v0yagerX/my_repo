# O. Ad-tech analogs, peer multiples and M&A precedents for MNTN (as of 2026-10-15)

**Scope.** This dossier covers live prices and multiples for AppLovin, The Trade Desk, Roku, Magnite, PubMatic, Viant, Criteo, DoubleVerify, Integral Ad Science, Pinterest and other relevant names. It also covers what each analog teaches about MNTN as an "AI victim" or "AI winner", and the CTV and performance ad-tech M&A record from 2022 to 2026.

**Status check.** MNTN has **not** reported Q3 2026. Its most recent 8-K is dated 2026-09-08 (Item 5.02), with Form 4s through 2026-10-07 [reported, SEC EDGAR submissions API, https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001891027.json]. Nothing in this dossier changes the Q2'26 baseline.

**Labels.** [reported] = company filing or exchange data. [guidance] = company outlook. [consensus] = sell-side average. These come from StockAnalysis.com (S&P Global data), an aggregator used only because no consensus terminal was available. Treat them as indicative. [third-party] = press or aggregator. [estimate] = my own derivation, with the math shown.

**Method notes.**
- Prices are intraday on 2026-10-15, about 14:00 ET, from the Interactive Brokers snapshot [S4].
- "NTM" calendarizes consensus: NTM = 0.21 × FY26 + 0.79 × FY27, because 2.5 of FY26's 12 months remain [estimate].
- NTM adj. EBITDA is my estimate (consensus margins were unavailable), except where guidance anchors it. Ranges are shown.
- EV = market cap − cash and investments + financial debt. Leases are excluded.

## 1. Summary

1. **The market has sorted ad tech into two camps in 2026, and MNTN is priced with the losers.**
   - **Down hard YTD** [reported, IBKR, S4]:
     - Buy-side and performance intermediaries whose AI or competitive moat was questioned: The Trade Desk **−65%**, AppLovin **−59%**, Criteo **−24%**, Teads **−43%**, Taboola **−24%**.
     - Pinterest **−13%** and MNTN **−14%**.
   - **Up YTD** [reported, IBKR, S4]:
     - Sell-side and CTV infrastructure, data platforms, and take-out targets: PubMatic **+100%**, Digital Turbine **+106%**, Zeta **+60%**, Magnite **+48%**, Roku **+43%** (Fox deal), DoubleVerify **+18%** (Nielsen deal).
   - For reference: S&P 500 (SPY) **+13.7%**, software ETF IGV **+3.0%** [reported, S4].
2. **MNTN trades at the bottom of the peer range despite top-quartile growth.**
   - **Valuation:** EV **≈ $0.53–0.57B** [estimate: $10.32 × 74.8–78.6M shares − $237.3M cash, no debt]. That is **~1.3–1.4x NTM revenue** and **~4.5–5.2x NTM adj. EBITDA** [estimate].
   - **Peer medians** (operating peers, excluding deal-pinned Roku and DV): **~2.15x NTM revenue and ~7.75x NTM EBITDA** [estimate, §3].
   - **Growth:** MNTN guides **+21% GAAP / ~24% ex-Maximum Effort** for FY26 [guidance, S1]. Consensus FY27 is **+18.5%** [consensus, S5]. Among the comps, only AppLovin, Zeta and Viant grow faster.
3. **The Trade Desk is the cautionary analog for "independent buy-side interface squeezed by walled gardens and agents".**
   - Revenue growth went from **+18% in FY25** to **+3% in Q2'26**, against its own guide of at least +8%. Q3'26 is guided to **≥ −12%** [reported/guidance, S10, S12, S13].
   - The 10-Q cites lower spend from existing clients and joint-business-plan discounts [reported, S11].
   - Over the same period Amazon's ad revenue grew **+26%** [reported, S19].
   - TTD then cut **~15% of staff** and called a special meeting to **reprice up to 15.6M underwater options** [reported, S16, S17].
   - It trades at **~1.8x NTM revenue and ~6x NTM EBITDA**, with consensus revenue falling in both FY26 and FY27 [estimate/consensus].
4. **AppLovin is the "AI winner" analog, but its 2026 collapse shows how fast an AI premium unwinds.**
   - Fundamentals are still exceptional: Q2'26 revenue **+53%**, **84%** adj. EBITDA margin [reported, S6].
   - The stock fell after management said "our pace of meaningful model improvement was lighter than normal" and the AI video creative tool was still a "work in progress." It closed **−19.66% on 2026-08-06** [third-party, plaintiff firm summary, S9; quotes in S7].
   - The CEO calls CTV "step three" and says the SMB long tail is "harder for us to make work right now," partly because SMBs lack TV-grade video creative [reported, S7]. **That gap is exactly MNTN's niche.**
5. **M&A prices are well above MNTN's public multiple.** [reported deal values; multiples are estimates, §6]
   - Nielsen–DoubleVerify: EV $2.15B, **~8.1x TTM adj. EBITDA / 2.8x TTM revenue**.
   - Novacap–IAS: $1.9B, **~9.0x FY25E EBITDA / 3.2x revenue**.
   - Mediaocean–Innovid: EV ~$500M, **3.3x TTM revenue**.
   - Fox–Roku: EV ~$22B, **4.4x TTM revenue**.
   - Pinterest–tvScientific: $465.1M, **~8–9x estimated net revenue**.
   - Walmart–Vibe: ~$1.2B, **~12x stale claimed ARR**.
   - Applying DV/IAS EBITDA multiples to MNTN gives **~$15–16/share** [estimate]. Applying Innovid/IAS revenue multiples gives **~$16/share**. The price is $10.32.

**Bottom line.** On this topic the *market* is classifying MNTN as an AI victim, valuing it near TTD and Criteo rather than with AppLovin, Zeta or Magnite. The *fundamental* analogs are mixed:
- MNTN shares TTD's structural weakness. It owns no inventory and has little proprietary audience data.
- It also has APP-like traits: a performance/ROAS orientation, a self-serve SMB funnel, AI creative, and an 80% gross margin on net revenue.

The valuation already embeds a TTD-like outcome. Strategic precedents set a takeout floor well above the current price.

## 4. Qualitative analogs: what MNTN shares and where it differs

### 4.1 AppLovin (APP): the "AI winner" template, and its 2026 stress test

**Why it is an AI winner** [reported, S6, S7]:
- Q2'26 revenue was $1,924M (+53%) and adj. EBITDA $1,614M (84%).
- Free cash flow was $863M in the quarter.
- Q3 guidance is $2,055–2,085M of revenue at an 83% EBITDA margin.
- The CEO: "Gaming is still the majority of our revenue, and the single biggest driver of its growth is model performance." Consumer/e-commerce "advertiser spend set another record, finishing 28% above Q4 2025 levels."
- Management "believe[s] this business can compound at roughly 30% annually."
- APP reinvests in training and inference compute ("every dollar of it is dependent on return").

**E-commerce self-serve expansion:**
- APP opened AppLovin Ads Manager to the public in Q2'26. The general-availability date was June 22, 2026, per a plaintiff summary [S9].
- Sequencing is "mid-market advertisers first ... with the long tail unlocking as our data compounds" [reported, S7].
- On SMBs: "If you think about that mid-market and up, probably has templates that match the creative needs on our platform. When you get into the SMBs that are signing up directly, they probably don't. And so, we've got to resolve that" [reported, S7].
- The SMB long tail is "harder for us to make work right now" [reported, S7].

**CTV via Wurl:**
- APP bought Wurl on 2022-04-01 for **$378.2M** ($219.3M cash, $137.4M stock, $21.5M present value of a deferred payment) [reported, S8].
- On Q2'26 CTV plans, the CEO said: "the first path to supply expansion will be on-device, and the second path will be connected TV ... We're just not at the point yet where we can go execute on it." Also: "Step three would be connected TV" [reported, S7].
- **No move into SMB performance TV is announced.**

**The 2026 drawdown** (−59% YTD; from a $738.01 peak to $261.00 low) [reported, S4]:
- Plaintiff firms allege a class period of Feb 12 to Aug 5, 2026 [third-party, Robbins LLP, 2026-10-01, S9]. These are allegations, not findings.
- They cite a BofA note (2026-07-13) that "AppLovin's eCommerce footprint expanded at a slower pace in June." The stock fell **−12.65%** to $442.85 [third-party, S9].
- They cite the Q2 print: revenue of $1.92B versus a $1.94B consensus. The stock fell **−19.66%** to $335.67 on 2026-08-06 [third-party, S9].
- Management attributed the quarter to a "pace of meaningful model improvement [that] was lighter than normal" [reported, S7].
- Later headlines, which I could not verify against primary sources [third-party, TheFly via StockAnalysis news page, S54; low confidence]:
  - Meta AI mobile-game creation tools (~Sep 25)
  - a court denying APP's restraining order against Unity (~Oct 2)
  - price-target cuts from Jefferies, BofA, Goldman, Morgan Stanley and others (Oct 8–14)
- APP still trades at **~11.5x NTM EBITDA** [estimate], so the AI premium has compressed rather than vanished.

**MNTN shares with APP:**
- Performance/ROAS positioning.
- A self-serve funnel for mid-market and SMB e-commerce and DTC advertisers.
- ML-driven bidding.
- High incremental margins (MNTN gross margin 80% [S1]).

**MNTN differs from APP:**
- **Inventory control.** APP owns mediation (MAX) and in-app supply relationships. MNTN buys third-party CTV inventory and does not control the auction.
- **Data scale.** Axon trains on billions of daily in-app events. MNTN's signal is its advertiser-site pixel and its own CTV outcomes.
- **Growth.** +53% versus +21%.
- **Margin.** 84% versus 26% adj. EBITDA.
- **Creative.** MNTN's QuickFrame and AI creative directly address the 30–60-second video gap APP's CEO describes [inference from S7].

**Read-across:**
1. APP is not yet a direct MNTN competitor in SMB CTV (low risk through 2027 by its CEO's own sequencing).
2. Wurl makes CTV entry credible later (2028+).
3. APP's de-rating shows the market pays a premium only while model improvement visibly compounds.

- Jeff Green: "This quarter did not meet the standard we set for ourselves" [S10].

**Mechanics** [reported, TTD Q2'26 10-Q, S11]:
- Growth came from "more overall advertising campaigns executed by new clients, partially offset by a decrease in gross spend from existing clients."
- Growth was also "partially offset by volume and other discounts in connection with joint business plans and other strategic partnerships." That is fee pressure via JBP discounts.
- Platform-operations costs rose 22% on supplier-provided data components and AI/hosting compute. Gross margin fell from ~78% to ~74% [estimate from S10].

**Walled-garden squeeze:**
- Amazon's advertising revenue was **$19.8B, +26%** in Q2'26 [reported, Amazon 8-K, S19].
- Amazon DSP has privileged integrations with Roku (~80M households), Netflix and Disney [third-party, Marketing Dive, S23].
- New Street cut its TTD target from $17 to $10, citing AWS and Google "Buyer Direct" disintermediation [third-party, MediaPost 2026-08-27, S18].
- TTD's product response is Kokai, plus Koa agents in "Kokai Zuma" (2026-08-27) (see dossier F).
- I found **no filing that attributes the shortfall to Kokai itself**, so none is asserted here.

**Organizational stress** [reported]:
- CFO change: Alex Kayyal out, interim CFO from 2026-01-24 [S14]. Two directors resigned in March 2026, triggering a Nasdaq audit-committee composition notice [S15].
- About 15% workforce reduction announced 2026-09-03, with $39–51M of charges [S16].
- Special meeting on 2026-10-19 to reprice up to **15,582,540** options with strikes of **$3.69–$122.59** to the closing price on the meeting date. The board cites underwater options "making the stock options a less effective means of incentivizing and retaining" staff [S17].

**Valuation:**
- The stock fell from a $55.11 52-week high to $13.16 (−65% YTD) [S4].
- It trades at **~1.8x NTM revenue and ~6x NTM EBITDA**, with consensus revenue down 5% in both FY26 and FY27 [estimate/consensus, S5].

**MNTN shares with TTD:**
- Independent buy-side software with no owned media.
- Net revenue recognition: MNTN "bill[s] our customers on a gross basis ... report[s] revenue on a net basis" [reported, MNTN 10-K, S2].
- Dependence on the same premium CTV supply that walled gardens are pulling into their own pipes.
- Exposure to agentic interfaces that commoditize the UI.
- Both stocks fell ~68–76% from their highs: TTD from a $55.11 52-week high to $13.16 (−76%); MNTN from a ~$32 Aug-2025 peak to $10.32 (−68%) [reported/estimate, S4 and baseline].

**MNTN differs from TTD:**
- **Customer base.** MNTN serves 4,225 SMB/mid-market self-serve advertisers. TTD serves agency holding companies, whose JBP leverage drives fee concessions. MNTN's customers have little negotiating power individually.
- **Growth.** MNTN +21% versus TTD +3%/−12%.
- **Product.** MNTN is a vertically integrated "Performance TV" product with creative, measurement and a guaranteed-visit model. TTD is a general-purpose DSP.
- **Scale.** TTD has ~9.5x MNTN's LTM revenue [estimate: TTD LTM $2,990M = 739.4 + 846.9 + 688.9 + 715.1 (SEC XBRL, S52) ÷ MNTN $313.3M]. That puts TTD in Amazon's direct line of fire. MNTN at ~0.4% of Amazon's quarterly ad revenue [estimate, dossier E] is below its radar.

**Read-across:**
1. The bear case for MNTN is a "TTD path": decelerating growth, fee compression and a de-rating to 1–2x revenue.
2. That multiple already describes MNTN today.

### 4.3 Roku (ROKU): the platform owner with an SMB self-serve push

**Results and deal:**
- Q2'26 total revenue was $1,354.7M (+22%). Platform revenue was $1,221.0M (+25%) and advertising $672.8M (+25%) [reported, S20].
- Adj. EBITDA was $254.3M (18.8%). TTM free cash flow was $704M [reported, S20].
- Fox is acquiring Roku for **$160/share ($96 cash + 0.9693 FOX shares), ~$22B EV**, announced 2026-06-15 [reported, S22]. Shareholders approved it on 2026-10-14/15 (Roku 8-K Item 5.07, 2026-10-15, EDGAR). DOJ review is ongoing and closing is expected in 1H27 [third-party, dossier G].
- Roku did not give an outlook [S20]. The stock trades at a ~3% spread to $160 [estimate].

**SMB push:**
- "Roku Ads Manager, our self-service ad platform for performance and SMB advertisers ... The number of advertisers using Ads Manager in Q1 more than doubled YoY" [reported, Q1'26 letter, S21].
- Roku targets "a meaningful share of the approximately $600 billion in annual SMB ad spend" [S21].
- Roku Ads Manager has a Shopify integration [S21]. Smartly was added as the first partner on the Roku Ads API in Q2 [S20].

**Amazon and other partners:**
- "Third-party DSPs accounted for nearly three-quarters of in-stream video ad spend" [S20].
- Roku is integrated with Amazon DSP, TTD, DV360 and Yahoo [S20, S21]. Amazon has authenticated reach on Roku's ~80M households [third-party, S23].

**MNTN shares with Roku:** both court the same SMB/performance advertiser with self-serve CTV, Shopify-style attribution and AI-lowered creative costs.

**MNTN differs from Roku:**
- Roku owns the OS, the home screen and the Roku Channel inventory.
- Roku has first-party household data across 100M+ streaming households.
- Roku recognizes O&O advertising revenue at a ~62% advertising gross margin [S20].
- MNTN is a buyer of that inventory (Roku is a "possible" supplier; see dossier D).

**Read-across:**
1. Roku is simultaneously a supplier, a competitor and, through Fox ownership, a potentially conflicted channel.
2. Its +43% YTD is deal-driven and says little about standalone CTV-ad-tech multiples.
3. The deal multiple (~4.4x TTM revenue) does set a strategic marker for CTV platforms with owned supply.

### 4.4 Magnite and PubMatic: SSPs exposed to CTV and DSP concentration

**Magnite** [reported, S24, S25, S26]:
- Q2'26 contribution ex-TAC was $189.6M (+17%). CTV ex-TAC was $97.1M (+36%).
- FY26 guidance was raised: ex-TAC +13–14%, adj. EBITDA margin ≥37%.
- **Concentration:** "In 2025, there were two buyers of advertising inventory that indirectly contributed to approximately 44% of revenue" [S26].
- Magnite repriced its $358M term loan on 2026-10-07 [S53].

**PubMatic** [reported, S27, S28]:
- Q2'26 revenue was $78.6M (+11%), "earlier than anticipated." Adj. EBITDA margin was 25%. About 60% of revenue comes from CTV, mobile app and "emerging" lines.
- AgenticOS has run "80+ fully autonomous" campaigns and 4,000+ AI deals. It claims "2x reach per dollar versus its incumbent DSP" for Level Agency.
- 10-K: "The Trade Desk and Google DV360 in particular – account for a significant portion of the ad impressions purchased on our platform."

**Market response:**
- PUBM +100% and MGNI +48% YTD [S4].
- I infer two drivers: CTV growth, and a "sell-side agentic infrastructure" narrative in which SSPs move closer to buyers.

**MNTN shares with the SSPs:** CTV exposure and AI and agentic product claims.

**MNTN differs from the SSPs:**
- MNTN sits on the buy side, as a *customer* of SSPs.
- SSPs monetize every DSP, including Amazon. They are hedged against buy-side share shifts. MNTN is not.
- Sell-side agents such as AgenticOS that sell directly to advertisers and agencies compete with the DSP layer, MNTN included.

**Read-across:** the market currently rewards **supply-side control** and penalizes **buy-side interfaces**. That is a structural headwind for MNTN's multiple.

  - Viant books revenue **gross**, so its 1.3x revenue multiple is not comparable to MNTN's net revenue. On ex-TAC it is ~2.2x [estimate].

**Criteo (CRTO):**

- It is OpenAI's first ad-tech partner, with **2,000+ brands advertising on ChatGPT** via Criteo [reported, S31].

**DoubleVerify / IAS:**
- Measurement and verification companies growing low single to mid-teens were taken out at **8–9x EBITDA**: by a strategic buyer (Nielsen) and by private equity (Novacap) [§6].

**Pinterest (PINS):**

## 8. Thesis implications

**Direction: mildly toward "AI victim" on structure, but the valuation already discounts a TTD-like outcome.** On a −5 (victim) to +5 (winner) scale, this topic scores **about −1 on fundamentals**. Valuation asymmetry is **positive**, so the investment read is more favorable than the fundamental read.

1. **Structural analog risk (negative).**
   - The 2026 tape rewards owners of supply and data (Roku, the SSPs, APP's mediation, Zeta's identity graph) and punishes buy-side interfaces (TTD, Criteo, Teads).
   - MNTN owns neither inventory nor a proprietary audience graph.
   - Its moat is the integrated SMB workflow (creative, buying and measurement). Agents and walled-garden self-serve tools are actively commoditizing that layer (see dossiers E and F).
2. **Growth and positioning (positive).**
   - Unlike TTD, MNTN is still growing ~20%+ with expanding margins. Its customers are SMBs with little negotiating power, so it is not exposed to holding-company JBP fee compression.
   - AppLovin's CEO explicitly cites the SMB TV-creative gap as unsolved, and his CTV plan is "step three." **MNTN's niche is not yet contested by the strongest AI performance engine.**
   - Pinterest/tvScientific and Walmart/Vibe are the nearer threats.
3. **Valuation (positive asymmetry).** At 4.5–5.2x NTM EBITDA and ~1.3x NTM revenue, MNTN sits:
   - **~35–40% below** the peer median;
   - at roughly TTD's multiple, despite ~25 points better growth;
   - **below every comparable take-out** (DV 8.1x, IAS ~9x, Innovid ~3.3x revenue).

   Precedent and peer frameworks cluster at **$12–17/share** [estimate], against $10.32. A Criteo-style "terminal decline" multiple would imply **~$5**. That is the bear-case anchor if growth stalls toward 0–5%.
4. **Watch items for Q3'26 (early Nov 2026):**
   - SMB/DR demand: Teads' suspended guidance and the cautious APP/PINS channel checks are yellow flags.
   - Whether MNTN's growth holds above ~20% while TTD prints negative.
   - Any sign of fee or take-rate pressure.
   - Progress on MCP and agentic features.
   - Any strategic interest, given the 2026 consolidation wave (Fox–Roku, Walmart–Vibe, Pinterest–tvScientific, Nielsen–DV, Paramount–WBD).
