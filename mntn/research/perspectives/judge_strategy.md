# MNTN: competitive-strategy verdict (7 Powers lens), 2027-2030

**Placement: At Risk. Score −1 (scale −2..+2). Confidence 55%.** The probabilities across the scale are Victim 20%, At Risk 55%, Neutral 20%, Beneficiary 5% and Winner ~0%.

MNTN's original edge was counter-positioning. Agencies, enterprise DSPs and linear TV sellers could not profitably serve small businesses that had never run TV ads with self-serve TV priced on results. AI has removed that constraint for the walled gardens, so the counter-positioning now works against MNTN. Its fee is opaque and bundled, at about 35–50% of gross spend [estimate, unverified; A, H]. That is the incumbent margin that near-zero-fee rivals driven by AI agents are attacking. Each point of take rate costs about 1.5 points of adjusted EBITDA margin [H; estimate]. No damage is visible yet, so this is not a Victim placement.

## Moat test

| Candidate moat | Power | Does it hold against AI? |
|---|---|---|
| Ease of use | Counter-positioning (legacy) | **No.** There are 10+ self-serve TV buying interfaces, and MCP lets AI agents drive any of them. Vibe shipped a read-write MCP first; its CEO claims 1,000+ brands use it [G, F] |
| Creative (QuickFrame AI) | None | **No.** It runs only on third-party models, and OpenAI removed the Sora API on 2026-09-24 [I]. The CEO calls it "table stakes" [B]. It feeds the funnel but is not a moat |
| Integrations/partners | Switching costs | **No.** The HubSpot app shows 100+ installs and 0 reviews. Adobe's CTV activation list omits MNTN [N; third-party] |
| Brand | Branding | **Weak.** It lowers acquisition cost but adds no pricing power. S&M is still 34.6% of revenue [F; Q2'26 10-Q] |
| Attribution (Verified Visits) | Process power | **Eroding to parity.** The 10-K reports "minimal patent protection", and MNTN has no MRC accreditation [N; 10-K FY25]. MNTN's own 8 new third-party measurement integrations put rivals on equal footing [M] |
| Conversion data and bidding | Data scale economies | **Partially.** 81M conversions from 2020–24 [N; 424B4] beat startups but lose to Walmart's and Amazon's closed-loop purchase data [G] |
| Supply relationships | Cornered resource (aggregation) | **Partially, and the strongest.** AI cannot create inventory rights. Over 99% of buying is through private deals, packaged for budgets (~$78K net revenue per customer per year) below direct-deal minimums [D, F; company claim/estimate]. It is not exclusive: buying runs through PubMatic, Magnite and third-party DSPs [D; 10-K], and suppliers are consolidating and selling direct |

Overall, no candidate gives MNTN both a benefit and a barrier against the rivals that matter. Supply aggregation and outcome data beat AI-native startups, but not the walled gardens.

## Strongest evidence for At Risk
1. **The counter-positioning has reversed.**
   - Amazon folded its DSP into its Ads Agent ("DVA+ ... to all advertisers") [E; Amazon page].
   - It reportedly charges 0% on its own media bought through programmatic guaranteed deals and about 1% on open-web inventory [H; third-party].
   - Roku Ads Manager advertisers more than doubled y/y [E; reported].
   - Universal Ads has no minimum spend and an MCP connector [G].
   - Walmart closed its purchase of Vibe, which has 10,000+ advertisers [F; reported].
2. **Switching costs are near zero.**
   - There are no committed contracts [A; 10-K], and the site says "no minimums" [M; company site].
   - Attribution data exports to about 11 third-party tools [E].
   - BuiltWith shows 7,513 expired MNTN tags against 5,804 live ones [M; third-party, low confidence].
3. **AI widened the funnel, not the business.**
   - QuickFrame AI had 73K+ signups in 1H26 [B; reported].
   - Yet net additions of trailing-12-month customers stayed at 242–351 a quarter, against 296–422 a year earlier [N; reported].
   - Growth in customers active within the quarter fell from +85% to +24% [C; 10-Q].
   - Northbeam's tracked spend for H1'26 showed Vibe +1,900% and MNTN +29% [M; third-party, published by Vibe].

## Strongest evidence against (toward Neutral)
1. **Nothing has broken where damage would show first.**
   - Gross margin is 80.3% [A; reported].
   - The allowance for customer credits and concessions is flat ($2.50M to $2.65M) [H; reported].
   - Receivables per dollar of revenue fell from 0.96 to 0.73, consistent with a stable take rate [H; estimate].
   - Q3 revenue is guided up 23–27% [guidance].
2. **Supply rights plus falling ad prices help MNTN.** The average streaming upfront CPM is $25.90, down from $27.75 [D; third-party]. MNTN can keep its fee while customers' returns improve. AI agents still need a TV-buying layer to call [F].
3. **The strongest AI ad engines are not in this niche yet.**
   - AppLovin calls CTV "step three" [O; reported].
   - Meta's Q2'26 call had zero TV mentions [E; reported].
   - About 95–97% of MNTN customers are new to TV [C], so MNTN mostly expands the category rather than fighting over it.
   - The share of smaller advertisers investing in CTV rose from 60% to 85% [G; third-party].

## What would move the placement one notch (next 4–8 quarters)
**Up to Neutral (0):**
- Customers active in the quarter growing ≥30%, net adds of ≥450 a quarter, and flat spend per active customer [C, N].
- A true net revenue retention figure, counting customers who left, of ≥110% [C].
- A disclosed take rate or gross billings, stable through FY27, with gross margin ≥78%.
- Disclosed spend sourced through AI agents or MCP, or QuickFrame-to-paying-customer conversion above 3% [N].
- Adobe or Google naming MNTN as a CTV activation partner [N].
- Exclusive sports deals, or confirmed YouTube or Prime Video access. About 38% of streaming time currently has no demonstrated access [D; estimate].

**Down to Victim (−2):**
- Q3'26 revenue at or below $87.5M, or an FY26 guidance cut toward $347M [B], then growth below 15% by 2H27.
- Gross margin below 75%, or new rebates, fee caps or rising concessions.
- Fox-Roku (closing 1H27) or Paramount-WBD restricting third-party small-business demand, or MNTN losing a major network or supply partner [D].
- Meta launching CTV ads for small businesses, or Amazon disclosing small-business streaming-TV adoption [E].
- S&M above about 35% of revenue with flat net adds, or the expansion rate falling below 110% [E].
