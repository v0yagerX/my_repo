**Verdict (financial and unit-economics lens): Neutral, leaning At Risk. Score −0.2. Confidence 55% in the Neutral bucket.**

Bucket probabilities: Victim 5%, At Risk 25%, Neutral 55%, Beneficiary 13%, Winner 2%. Weighted, that comes to about −0.18.

### Where the growth comes from
- **Q2'26:** PTV revenue grew +20.9%. Customers active in the quarter grew +24% [reported; C, Q2'26 10-Q], and spend per active customer fell about −2.5% [estimate; C].
  - For 1H26 the spend decline is about −3%, or about −4% excluding the $1.7M creative-credit breakage (C, verification).
- **2025:** customers grew +85% and spend per customer fell −21% to −27% [reported/estimate; C].
- So the whole slowdown from +36% to +21% ex-Maximum Effort growth [reported; A] is in customer acquisition. Spend per customer has stabilized.
- TTM revenue per TTM customer ($74.2K, −8.7%) [estimate; C] lags and overstates the dilution.

### Metrics already showing AI benefit (cost side, and mostly used up)
- **Gross margin:** 76.8% to 80.3% y/y in Q2, with cost of revenue up only +2% against revenue up +20.6% [reported; A].
  - Cost-of-revenue headcount fell 38% y/y and hosting costs fell $2.7M in 1H26 [reported; B]. Crediting AI for this is an inference (B, verification).
  - Gross margin already sits above the 75–80% long-term target and has slipped for two quarters (82.1%, 81.5%, 80.3%) [reported; A, H]. There is no upside left here.
- **Headcount:** 542 to 451 after the April 2026 layoff, about $695K TTM revenue per employee [reported/estimate; B, L].
- **Profit and cash:** adjusted EBITDA margin rose from 21.2% to 26.1% (Q2 y/y). LTM FCF is $59.6M (19.0%) [estimate; A]. Net cash is $237.3M with no debt [reported; A].
- **No visible take-rate damage:**
  - Concession allowances were flat at $2.50M to $2.65M a year, under 1% of revenue [reported; H, 10-K].
  - Receivables per dollar of quarterly revenue fell from 0.96 to 0.73 [estimate; H]. The caveat is that a shift to card-paying customers could cause this (A, verification).

### Metrics consistent with AI damage (not proven)
- **Customer growth:** quarterly-active customer growth fell from +85% to +24% [reported; C].
  - Management blames its own sales and marketing rebuild and deliberate pacing of small-business onboarding [B].
  - Cash S&M excluding SBC was roughly flat y/y in Q2'26 ($23.6M to $24.5M, including about $1.1M of severance) [estimate from reported; A]. Marketing spend fell $1.3M [reported; B].
  - That fits a self-inflicted slowdown, but it does not rule out the big ad platforms' AI self-serve tools capturing the small-business funnel.
- **S&M efficiency:** incremental PTV revenue per S&M dollar fell from 0.85x (FY25) to 0.72x (TTM Q2'26) [estimate; C].
- **T&D spend:** up +52% y/y, from 15.7% to 19.8% of revenue [reported/estimate; A]. AI is a rising cost of staying in place:
  - The CEO calls model orchestration "table stakes" [B].
  - A new 2026 risk factor warns rivals may build "similar or superior" AI more cheaply [A].
- **Concentration:** top-10 customers rose from 17% to 20% of revenue [reported; C].

### Metrics that are silent
- **Take rate and gross billings:** estimated at 35–50%; the receivables-based ~46% is probably an upper bound [estimate/third-party; A].
  - That compares with 21.6% at The Trade Desk [estimate; H].
  - Each take-rate point is worth about 1.7–2.2% of revenue and about 1.5 points of EBITDA margin [estimate; H].
- **Retention:** no gross retention or churn data. There is only a survivor-based "expansion rate well north of 115%" [reported, qualitative; C].
- **AI revenue:**
  - QuickFrame AI has 73K+ signups but earns $0.
  - Express has "hundreds" of paying customers, under 2% of revenue [reported/estimate; B].
- **Other:** no CAC figures and no win/loss data against Amazon or tvScientific.

### Why Neutral and not At Risk
- Every reported line that would show fee pressure or AI substitution is flat or better: gross margin, concessions, receivables, and spend per active customer.
- Growth is self-funded. TTM SBC is 7.2% of revenue, and FCF after SBC is about $37M [estimate; A, L].

### Why Neutral and not Beneficiary
- The AI benefit so far is on the cost side and has largely been harvested. AI products on the revenue side earn almost nothing.
- The one growth driver slowed sharply, and the cause is unknown.
- 2027–30 value rests on an undisclosed spread about twice The Trade Desk's, while DSP fees are under pressure across the industry [H].

At $10.25, the model's reverse DCF implies 0–7% revenue CAGR for FY27–30 [estimate; model v1]. The market is pricing At Risk; the reported numbers do not yet show it.
