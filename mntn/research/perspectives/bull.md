# MNTN long pitch: priced as an AI casualty, operating like an AI beneficiary

**$10.25 | EV about $529M | base $16-18 | bull $25-33 | bear $5-7 | Q3 print Nov 4**

## Variant perception
The market treats MNTN as the next Trade Desk: a middleman that Amazon, Google and AI agents will cut out. It trades at about 5.4x FY26 adj. EBITDA guidance and 4.3x FY27E. Net cash is 31% of the market cap [K; estimate].

Our reverse DCF uses FCF after SBC, an 11% cost of equity and 3% terminal growth. At $10.25 it implies FY27-30 revenue CAGR of **+0.6% at a 30% 2030 margin, and -2.7% at 33.5%** [model v1; estimate]. Compare that with FY27 consensus of +18.5% [K; consensus] and a Q3 guide of +23-27% [B; guidance]. The price assumes no growth, yet the company shows none of the damage the market fears. AI helps MNTN on balance. It removes the main thing holding small businesses back, which is not having a TV commercial, and it is already cutting costs.

## Pillars
**1. The numbers show no AI damage; the slowdown came from customer mix, and that effect is now in the base.**
- Revenue has beaten or matched consensus in 5 of 5 quarters [K; consensus].
- Gross margin is 80% [A; reported]. Concessions are flat at under 1% of revenue [H; reported].
- Receivables per revenue dollar fell from 0.96 to 0.73. That fits a flat or rising take rate [H; estimate].
- Growth fell from 46% to 21% because MNTN deliberately moved down-market:
  - Spend per in-period active customer fell 21-27% in 2025, but only about 3% in 1H26 [C; estimate].
  - Revenue now tracks active-customer growth (+24%) [C; reported].

**2. The moat is in things AI can't copy.**
- AI cannot create premium TV inventory. More than 99% of MNTN's buying is private-marketplace deals [D; CEO]. That includes Disney live inventory, plus Paramount and NBCU inventory through SSPs [D; reported].
- MNTN adds conversion-trained bidding and Verified Visits. These are backed by holdout tests and 8 third-party measurement integrations [M; reported].
- It is neutral. Advertisers do not have to let Amazon or Walmart grade results on their own platforms.

**3. Owner-operators with a fortress balance sheet.**
- $237.3M net cash and no debt [A; reported]. TTM FCF is $59.6M, a 7.8% yield [K; estimate].
- The $100M buyback covers about 13% of shares [L; estimate].
- No executive has sold since the IPO. The CEO's 1M-share 10b5-1 plan has no executions [L; reported].
- Qualcomm's 5.1M-share overhang is down to 147K [L; reported].

**4. Scarcity value.**
- Walmart paid about $1.2B for Vibe [G; third-party, WSJ], roughly 2x MNTN's EV.
- Pinterest paid $465.1M for tvScientific [G; reported].
- MNTN is the last independent of scale. Its CEO says the company would hear a legitimate approach [G; third-party].

## What the market misses about AI
- **AI widens the market.**
  - 95-97% of customers have never run a TV ad [C; management].
  - AI cuts the cost of a finished spot to about $24-600 [I; estimate], against a cited $10K-500K [I; third-party, low confidence].
  - CTV use among smaller advertisers rose from 60% to 85% from 2024 to 2026 [G; third-party].
  - MNTN already takes customers from making the ad to airing it: 73K+ QuickFrame AI signups in 1H26 and 7K+ Express signups in 120 days [B; reported].
  - AppLovin, the strongest AI ad engine, calls CTV "step three" [O; reported].
- **AI cuts costs.** Headcount fell 4.4% y/y while revenue grew 21% [M; reported]. New job postings target "AI deflection" in customer support [M; reported].
- **AI agents become a sales channel.**
  - Agents need tools to call: supply, attribution and bidding. MNTN has a read-write MCP server in beta [F; reported].
  - S&M is about 30% of revenue excluding SBC [F; estimate]. Any cut in customer acquisition cost from agents goes straight to margin.

## Valuation
- **Peers.** The peer median is about 7.75x NTM EBITDA; MNTN is at about 4.5-5.2x. DV and IAS were acquired at about 8-9x EBITDA. Peer and deal multiples imply **$12-17/share** [O; estimate].
- **Model** [model v1; estimate]:
  - Base case: 7x FY28 EBITDA ($147M) at end-2027 gives $18.26, or $16.09 in today's money. The DCF gives $16.15.
  - Probability-weighted (30/50/20 bear/base/bull): $14.58-17.96.
- **Street.** The mean analyst target is $18.10 [K; consensus].
- **Asymmetry.** About +57% to +78% to the base case, against about -34% to -52% to the bear case. The bear case needs revenue to *shrink* from 2028.

## Catalysts (4-8 quarters)
1. **Nov 4.** Q3 against $87.7M consensus and Q4 implied at about $108M [K; consensus]. Also the first FY27 commentary, and buyback execution in the 10-Q.
2. **Dec 15, 2026.** The CEO's 10b5-1 plan expires. Letting it expire without a sale would show alignment [L; reported].
3. **Feb 2027 FY27 guide** against $417M consensus. The stock rose 37% the last time guidance beat consensus [K; third-party].
4. **2027.** Management expects Express and QuickFrame to contribute "meaningfully" [B; management]. Margins move toward the 35-40% target; Q4'26 is implied at about 34% [B; estimate].
5. **Consolidation.** Fox-Roku closes in 1H27 [E; reported]. Because Class B holders control about 66% of the vote, any deal would have to be friendly [L; reported].

## Sell if
- Q3 comes in below $87.5M, or FY26 guidance is cut toward $347M [B].
- Spend per active customer falls more than 5% again while active-customer growth slips under 20%.
- Gross margin falls below 78%, or receivables per revenue dollar or concessions rise [H].
- The expansion rate drops below 110% [E], or MNTN loses a major network or SSP [D].
- The buyback is not executed by Q4, executives sell, or Meta launches CTV ads for SMBs [E].
- Trim at about $18 unless FY27 estimates rise.
