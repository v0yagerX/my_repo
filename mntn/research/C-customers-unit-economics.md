## 1. Bottom line

1. **The headline gap (TTM customers +40% vs revenue +21%) is mostly a measurement artifact in 2026, but it was real in 2025.** Alongside the trailing-12-month (TTM) count, the 10-Qs give the growth in customers active *within the period*. In Q2'26 that figure was **+24%** y/y. Q1'26 was **+26%** and 1H26 was **+27%** [reported] ([S11] p. MD&A; [S9]). Against PTV revenue growth of about **+21%** [estimate, below], spend per in-period active customer fell only about **2–3%** in 1H26 [estimate]. In 2025 the gap was real and large. In-period customers grew **+85–89%** while PTV revenue grew **+32–48%**, so spend per active customer fell about **21–27%** [estimate]. Management attributes that fall to the push into small businesses.
2. **In-period customer acquisition has slowed sharply**, from +85–89% (1H25) to +24–26% (1H26). The TTM headline (+40%) lags because it still counts customers who were active, including paid-trial churners, at any time in the past 12 months. Management says the pace is "largely within our control" and depends on S&M intensity [reported] [S12].
3. **Revenue per customer is structurally lower and still drifting down, but the decline is slowing.**
   - TTM revenue ex-Maximum Effort per TTM customer fell from **$94.1K** (Q4'24) to **$74.2K** (Q2'26) [estimate].
   - On a PTV basis the path is about **$106K** (FY23) → **$92K** (FY24) → **$78K** (FY25) → **$74K** (TTM Q2'26) [estimate].
   - The y/y decline moderated from about −16% to −18% through 2025 to about **−8% to −10%** in 1H26 [estimate].
4. **The mix shift is deliberate and documented.** Small businesses rose from about **6%** of revenue (Q4'24) to **12%** of PTV revenue (Q1'25) and about **15%** (Q3'25) [reported] ([S1]; [S14]). MNTN Express, an entry tier for small businesses, launched on 2026-04-01. It had more than **7,000** signups in 120 days, of which "hundreds" became paying [reported] [S12]. The next leg will therefore dilute revenue per customer again.
5. **Existing-customer health looks good on the limited data available.**
   - The "expansion rate" is "well north of 115%" in every quarter since Q3'25 [reported] ([S12], [S13], [S14], [S16]). It is a survivor-based metric, though: current customers versus the same customers a year ago.
   - The last true NRR disclosed was SMB NRR of **108%** (FY24) and **111%** (Q1'25) [reported] [S1].
   - Top-10 customer share is rising, from **17%** (FY25) to **20%** (1H26) [reported] ([S7], [S11]).
   - No churn or cohort data has ever been disclosed. The S-1 contains no cohort chart.
6. **S&M efficiency is adequate but not exceptional.**
   - Incremental TTM PTV revenue per TTM S&M dollar was about **0.71x** (FY24), **0.85x** (FY25) and **0.72x** (TTM Q2'26) [estimate].
   - TTM S&M per net new TTM customer was about **$64K–$95K** [estimate].
   - The only CAC claim from management is "LTV CAC ratios that are handily in the double digits on the majority of our mid-market customers" [reported] [S13]. No numbers back it up.
7. **First-time TV advertisers** are about **95–97%** of customers, and this has been disclosed repeatedly [reported] ([S1], [S2], [S4], [S7], [S16]).

## 2. Every disclosed customer metric since the S-1

### 2.1 Definitions (quoted)

- **PTV Customers / Active PTV Customers (TTM):** "PTV Customers refers to the aggregate number of unique customers that use our PTV platform as part of their CTV campaigns in the twelve-month period preceding the date indicated" [reported] (FY25 10-K [S7]; same wording in the 424B4 [S1]).
- **Customer:** "any marketer that uses our solutions and platform, either directly or through a third-party agency" [reported] [S7]. The count is therefore at marketer/brand level, not agency level.
- **Paid trials are counted:** "Our customer count includes any customer that uses the platform and is billed, including during trial periods, which we define as three months or fewer, and customers who continue to use the platform post-trial. Churn during paid trial periods is primarily due to limited experience using performance marketing platforms." [reported] ([S1] p. 102; [S7] Business). **This is the key definitional fact.** A trial customer who churns after three months or less stays in the TTM count for up to four quarters.
- **SMB Net Revenue Retention Rate (S-1 only):** revenue in a subsequent 12-month period from "all SMB customers who used the platform and were billed for more than three months during a prior twelve-month period," divided by that cohort's prior-period revenue [reported] [S1]. This is a true NRR because churned customers count at zero.
- **"Expansion rate" (post-IPO):** "measures the spend of our current customers as compared to those same customers' spend a year ago" (CFO Patrick Pohlen, Q2'26 call) [reported] [S12]. **It is not an NRR.** It covers only *current* customers, so churned customers are excluded and the figure is structurally higher than NRR.
- **"Total Customers"** is defined in the S-1 [S1], but no count was ever reported.

## 4. Diagnosis: why are customers +40% while revenue is +21–25%?

Gap arithmetic for Q2'26 [estimate]:
- TTM customers +39.9% vs PTV revenue +20.9% gives a gap of about **19 points**.
- In-period customers +24% vs revenue +20.9% gives a gap of about **3 points**.
- So about **16 of the 19 points** (~80–85%) of the 2026 gap is the TTM-vs-in-period effect. The remaining ~3 points is a genuine fall in spend per active customer.
- In 2025 the gap was mostly genuine dilution: in-period customers +85% vs revenue +35%.

| Hypothesis | Evidence for | Evidence against / caveats | My weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(e) Definitional / TTM lag, plus one-off and trial customers** | (1) The TTM count includes paid-trial customers (three months or less) who churn, and they stay in the count for up to 12 months [S1], [S7]. (2) In-period growth was +24% vs TTM +40% in Q2'26 [S11]. (3) Q2'26 TTM net adds rebounded to +351 from +242. That quarter included the Express launch, with "hundreds" of paying customers [S12], and first-time FIFA World Cup / premium-sports placements [S10]. Event advertisers stay in the TTM count through Q1'27. (4) The company switched its headline metric from in-quarter (Q2'25) to TTM (Q3'25 onward) [S2], [S4]. | The number of World Cup-only advertisers is not disclosed. | **Dominant explanation for the 2026 gap (~80%)** |
| **(a) Deliberate shift to smaller customers / new entry tiers** | (1) Every 10-Q/10-K revenue paragraph since the IPO says growth was "offset by a decrease in average spend per customer as we continued to expand our overall SMB footprint, including small businesses" [S3], [S5], [S7], [S9], [S11]. **Verdict.** Through 2025 the gap was a real dilution of revenue per customer, driven by the deliberate move into small businesses (a) and amplified by paid-trial churners in the TTM count (e). In 2026 most of the remaining gap is a TTM lag artifact (e). Underlying spend per active customer has roughly stabilized (−0% to −3% y/y) while the real customer-growth engine slowed to about +25%. For the model, forecast **in-period active customers** and spend per active customer, not the TTM headline.

Forward implication [estimate]: the TTM count should keep converging toward in-period growth. Holding sequential TTM adds at +250 to +400 gives y/y TTM growth of about **35–40% in Q3'26** (4,475–4,625 vs 3,316) and about **30–38% in Q4'26** (4,725–5,025 vs 3,632). After that it should trend toward the mid-20s unless Express adds thousands of paying accounts. Express customers will be small, so revenue per TTM customer should keep falling by roughly **5–10% per year** if Express scales as management hopes ("2027 to really start to see the larger impact") [S12].

Caveats:
1. S&M also pays for account management and expansion, so S&M per net add **overstates** true CAC.

On balance, MNTN acquires customers at roughly 0.7–0.85x revenue per S&M dollar. That is decent for a usage-based model with 80% gross margin, but efficiency fell from FY25 to TTM Q2'26 as S&M was re-accelerated. ## 7. AI angle (as it bears on customers)

- AI creative removes the main SMB onboarding barrier: "more than 95% of our customers don't have [a TV commercial] when launching their first campaign" [S13]. QuickFrame AI had **~37,000 signups in Q2'26 and 73,000+ YTD**. It is free and "essentially gross margin neutral" [reported] [S12]. Many signups are "creators … not necessarily the marketers", so the funnel to paying PTV customers is unproven [S12].
- MNTN Express is aimed at small businesses: 7,000+ signups and "hundreds" paying in 120 days [reported] [S12]. **[estimate]** That is a signup-to-paid conversion in the **low single-digit to ~10%** range ("hundreds" ÷ 7,000 ≈ 1.4–14%, depending on what "hundreds" means).
- Agentic AI was added for mid-market customers [S12].

On this topic, AI mainly widens the top of the funnel and lowers onboarding cost. Its side effect is to pull revenue per customer *down*, because the new customers are small and low-budget.

## 9. Thesis implications

- **The model should be driven by in-period active customers and spend per active customer, not TTM customers.** In-period growth of ~24–26% with about −2% spend per customer is consistent with Q3'26 guidance of +23–27% y/y [guidance] [S10] and FY26 ~24% ex-ME growth [guidance] [S10].
- **Bull read:**
  - Spend per active customer has stopped falling.
  - The survivor expansion rate is >115% and top-10 share is rising, so existing customers are healthy.
  - Customer growth is a dial management controls through S&M.
  - Express, AI creative and premium sports inventory could re-accelerate adds in 2027.
- **Bear read:**
  - In-period customer growth fell by about 60 points in a year.
  - The headline TTM metric flatters it and will mechanically decelerate toward the mid-20s or lower.
  - Retention is undisclosed, and the post-IPO metric excludes churners.
  - S&M efficiency fell as S&M re-accelerated in 1H26.
  - The next wave (Express) is structurally lower-ARPU and unproven.
- **AI victim or winner, on this topic:** a **modest tilt toward "AI winner"** (roughly +1 on a −5 to +5 scale, low-to-medium confidence). Generative-AI creative (QuickFrame AI) and AI targeting/optimization address the binding constraint for SMB TV advertisers, which is having no TV ad. That expands the customer funnel at low marginal cost, and MNTN's customer base (95–97% new to TV) is exactly the population AI tools unlock. However, the evidence that AI-sourced small customers retain and monetize is missing. Revenue per customer will keep falling, and AI equally lowers the barrier for larger self-serve competitors to reach the same SMBs. The cohort and retention data in a future filing, or at an investor day, is the single most important thing to watch.

**Net assessment.** None of the 14 load-bearing or numeric findings was refuted, and every reported number matched the primary SEC documents exactly. The derived arithmetic reproduces to within rounding. The points to carry forward are:

1. **1H26 spend per in-period customer** is about −3% (−4% ex-breakage), not −2.5%. The −2.5% is the Q2'26 figure.
2. **The "mid-sized 86% (FY24)" reading is ambiguous.** Read literally, the 424B4 gives 86% of SMB revenue, or about 79% of PTV.
3. **Two items labeled [reported] are partly estimates:** the Express conversion rate and the Q2'26 ~$19.4K "ARPU".
4. **The "~16 of 19 points definitional" split is arithmetic, not evidence of cause.** Part of the TTM-vs-in-period gap reflects a real slowdown in customer acquisition, not only trial churn.
