## 3. Lock-up and follow-on offerings

- **Lock-up terms** [reported, 424B4]: the restriction ran until the **earlier of (i) the open of trading on the 2nd trading day after the earnings release for "the second quarter following the most recent period" in the prospectus (Q1'25, so Q3'25), and (ii) 180 days after May 21, 2025 (Nov 17, 2025)**. It covered about **67.4M shares** of holders.
- **Expiry:** Q3'25 results were released Nov 4, 2025 (8-K 0001891027-25-000019), so the **lock-up lapsed at the open on Nov 6, 2025** [estimate from the formula]. Corroboration: Baroda converted its 6,711,098 Class B to Class A on **Nov 6, 2025** (Form 4 0001891027-25-000024) [reported].
- **Post-lock-up conversions:** 10,954,138 Class B converted to Class A in 2H25 [reported, 10-K Note 12]. That figure exactly equals Baroda 6,711,098 + Rincon/Bonfire 3,243,040 + 1,000,000 from MGD Holdings [estimate: the reconciliation ties to the share]. A further 5,126,715 converted in 1H26 (Qualcomm) [reported].
- **Follow-ons:** **none.** EDGAR shows no S-1, S-3, 424B2/B3/B5 or underwritten secondary since the IPO 424B4. MNTN became S-3 eligible in about May 2026 (12 months of reporting) but has filed no shelf [reported, absence]. The only post-IPO registrations are the S-8s of May 22, 2025 and Feb 19, 2026 (evergreen).

## 4. Share classes, voting control, holders and float

### 4a. Structure [reported, 10-K FY25 Note 12; 424B4]
- **Class A: 1 vote. Class B: 10 votes**, convertible 1:1, and converting automatically on most transfers.
- **Sunset:** all Class B converts at the earliest of (i) 61-180 days after Class B falls **below 5% of total shares outstanding** (currently 12.91M / 74.80M = **17.3%** [estimate]); (ii) the **7th anniversary of IPO closing (about May 23, 2032)**; or (iii) a vote of 66⅔% of Class B.
- The pre-IPO voting agreement **terminated at the IPO** [reported, 424B4]. MNTN is **not a controlled company**: the board has 6 of 7 directors independent [reported, DEF 14A].
- At the IPO, Class B held **86.4%** of the vote, with Douglas at about 16.7% [reported, 424B4 cover]. After the 2H25-1H26 conversions, Class B holds **about 66% of the vote** [estimate: 129.1M Class B votes ÷ about 194.5M total votes].
- Annual meeting (Jun 11, 2026): 46,424,382 Class A + 12,910,630 Class B were present, which is 175,530,682 votes or 90.38% of voting power [reported, 8-K Jun 17, 2026, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1891027/000189102726000060/mntn-20260611.htm].

## 8. Implications for the AI-victim vs AI-winner question

1. **Insider behavior is not that of an AI victim.** The CEO, CFO and COO have sold **nothing** since the IPO, despite a 10b5-1 plan (CEO, 1M sh) that has been in place since Sep 2025 [reported]. The CEO carries about $80M of exposure at $10.24 [estimate]. Sellers have been **financial or strategic pre-IPO holders** (Qualcomm about 4.98M sh, Baroda 0.34M, director Partovi 0.45M) monetizing a 2012-2023 investment. That is consistent with VC fund-life liquidity, not necessarily with negative information.
2. **The capital structure supports per-share compounding.**
   - SBC fell from 10.9% of revenue in FY25 to about 7% TTM [estimate].
   - Basic share growth is about 2%/yr.
   - A $100M buyback (about 13% of shares) plus about $237M of net cash gives management scope to **shrink the share count** in 2027-2030 if FCF keeps growing [estimate].
   - The April 2026 RIF (about 14% of staff) fits an "AI-leveraged opex" narrative that the CEO explicitly ties to engineering productivity [third-party transcript].
3. **Offsets:**
   - (i) **About 44% of shares sit with pre-IPO VCs and strategics**, against about 1M sh/day of volume. That is a supply overhang regardless of fundamentals.
   - (ii) The **$10M/yr all-stock contractor deal** pushes SBC back up and is opaque (counterparty unnamed).
   - (iii) **Dual-class control persists to 2032**, and two holders (Douglas plus Greycroft) hold about 53% of the vote.
   - (iv) Short interest of about 9-10% of float shows that a meaningful cohort is betting on the bear case. That case includes AI-driven self-serve CTV tools at Amazon, Google and Meta compressing MNTN's SMB niche.
4. **Net:** this topic leans mildly toward **"not a victim" at the equity-structure level**. Insiders are aligned, dilution is modest and buyback firepower is large. It does not settle the AI question by itself. Execution of the buyback, shown in the Q3 10-Q on about Nov 9, 2026, is the next hard data point.
