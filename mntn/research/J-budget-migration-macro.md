## 0. Bottom line

1. **AI assistant and AI search ads are now real and growing very fast, but they are still small and so far come mainly from search and e-commerce budgets, not TV.**
   - ChatGPT ads reached a **$1B annualized run-rate in under 200 days**, with "tens of thousands of advertisers" [reported by OpenAI via MediaPost, 2026-08-31].
   - OpenAI's own plan calls for **$2.5B in 2026 → ~$100B by 2030** [third-party: Axios, 2026-04-09, citing investor materials].
   - WPP Media puts generative-search ads at **$5.1B globally in 2026**, about 0.4% of all ad revenue, growing at a ~100% CAGR to more than $100B by 2030 [third-party, 2026-06-15]. WPP says the money comes from "traditional search" and e-commerce budgets.
   - Meanwhile the incumbent performance pools are still growing fast: Google Search **+17%** and Meta advertising **+27%** in Q2 2026 [reported]. The pie is expanding, not yet being re-cut.
2. **The medium-term risk to MNTN is real but second-order.**
   - MNTN's own go-to-market targets "marketers currently using paid search and social channels" [reported, 10-K FY2025]. AI assistant ads therefore compete for the same performance budget and the same SMB "experiment" money.
   - My estimate of the drag: **~0 to 1 pp/yr of revenue growth in a base case over 2027-2030, and 2-3 pp/yr in a bear case** [estimate, §3.4].
   - Near-term (2026-27) impact is negligible.
3. **Macro is the bigger and nearer risk than AI.** MNTN's exposure:
   - About **91-92% of Performance TV revenue comes from SMBs** [reported, 424B4].
   - "Almost all" customers have no committed contracts [reported, 10-K].
   - The customer base skews to e-commerce and travel [reported via AdExchanger].
   - Small-business sentiment is soft: the NFIB index was **97.9 in Sept 2026**, below its 52-year average [reported]. U. Michigan consumer sentiment hit a **record-low 44.8 in May 2026** [reported, FRED]. CPI is reaccelerating to **+3.7% y/y** [reported, FRED].
   - Hard spending data is still positive: real PCE **+2.6% y/y** in August [reported/estimate].
   - Ad-market forecasts for 2026 were raised (IAB **+12.3%**, WPP US **+11.9% ex-political**) [third-party].
   - In a 2027 ad recession, history (Yelp −32% in Q2 2020; TTD −13%; Google Search −10%) points to MNTN growth falling from ~20-25% to roughly **−10% to +10% for the trough year** [estimate, §5.4].
4. **One-offs are mostly benign:**
   - **Midterms:** MNTN says it has little political exposure and buys >99% of inventory through private marketplaces at pre-negotiated prices [reported, Q2'26 call]. Its revenue grew ~34% in Q4 2024 during a record presidential cycle [estimate from filings]. The 2027 comp is therefore not inflated by political revenue.
   - **Maximum Effort:** the divestiture distortion ended after Q1 2026. It depresses FY2026 GAAP growth by ~2.3 pp [estimate].
   - **World Cup:** an inventory feature, not a disclosed revenue driver.

Key takeaways:

- **OpenAI is building exactly the SMB-friendly stack that MNTN sells:**
  - self-serve buying;
  - CPC (outcome-like) bidding;
  - a conversions API;
  - agency and ad-tech partners.
- In under 200 days ChatGPT signed up "tens of thousands" of advertisers [reported]. That is several times MNTN's **4,225** active trailing-twelve-month Performance TV customers [reported, Q2'26]. MNTN Express, MNTN's own low-end self-serve tier, drew **7,000+ signups in 120 days, with "hundreds" paying** [reported, Q2'26 call via Motley Fool transcript].
- This shows SMBs will readily test a new AI channel. Their test budgets are finite.

Early evidence on ChatGPT ad efficacy is anecdotal and mixed. In MediaPost (2026-08-31):

- one agency reported a 108% CTR increase and 19% lower CPC after OpenAI's "Max Results" bidding [third-party, anecdote];
- another reported about 0.6% CTR versus about 2% for comparable search ads, with no buyers [third-party, anecdote].

eMarketer's Nate Elliott called OpenAI's $2.5B 2026 target "all but impossible" at the current pace [third-party, MediaPost 2026-08-31].

**My 2026 estimate for ChatGPT ad revenue: ~$0.6-0.8B globally** [estimate]:

- I assume a roughly linear ramp from $0 at launch (Feb 9) to a $1B run-rate on Aug 31. That averages ~$0.5B annualized over ~0.56 years, or ≈ $0.28B.
- For September to December I assume a run-rate averaging ~$1.2-1.6B, times 0.33 years, or ≈ $0.4-0.5B.
- Total: ≈ $0.7B ± $0.1B.

## 3. Where does AI-ad money come from: search/social or TV?

### 3.1 Evidence

- **Forecasters say mainly search and commerce.** WPP's Kate Scott-Dawkins says advertisers will shift "traditional search" and e-commerce budgets into generative-AI ad options. Some spending "transitions from traditional search" [third-party, Digiday 2026-06-16, https://digiday.com/media-buying/wpp-forecasts-ai-search-ads-to-become-fastest-growing-channel-in-advertising/]. eMarketer frames AI search as a cannibalization risk to keyword search revenue [third-party, MediaPost 2025-06-04].
- **Incumbent pools are still growing fast, so there is no visible reallocation yet:**
  - Google Search & other **+17%** (Q2'26 $63.271B vs $54.190B) [reported, Alphabet 8-K];
  - YouTube ads **+13%**;
  - Google Network **−0.7%** ($7.303B vs $7.354B) [reported].
  - Meta ads **+27%** [reported].
  - Roku advertising **+25%** [reported, Roku Q2'26 letter].
  - US streaming is forecast at **+21.5%** in 2026 [third-party, WPP].
- **MNTN's own budget sources overlap with AI-ad targets:**
  - The 10-K says MNTN "focus[es] sales efforts on marketers currently using paid search and social channels as well as existing TV marketers" [reported, 10-K FY2025, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1891027/000189102726000019/mntn-20251231.htm].
  - Risk factors warn that revenue suffers if marketers "dedicate budgets to alternative marketing channels such as paid search or paid social" [reported].
  - The 10-K does **not** name AI assistants or LLM ads as a competitive channel. That is a disclosure gap.
  - An older MNTN/Digiday survey of 123 marketers found **58% shifting budget from linear to CTV and 41% from paid social** [third-party, MNTN-sponsored, 2023; snippet via eMarketer sponsored content].
- **SMB channel mix** (LocaliQ, 730+ SMBs, updated 2025-12-03) [third-party, https://localiq.com/blog/small-business-marketing-trends-report/]:
  - channel use: unpaid social 52%, social ads 47%, search ads 40% (27% for budgets under $1k/month), online listings 28%;
  - "nearly 60%" use AI in marketing;
  - 49% planned to raise 2025 budgets, 35% to hold and 16% to cut (56% of those cutting cited the economy);
  - TV and print "have fallen lower" as lead sources;
  - ChatGPT and AI search were not yet measured as an ad channel.
- **MNTN customer mix:**
  - in 2024, **92% of PTV revenue came from SMBs, "of which 86% was from mid-sized businesses"** (≈79% of PTV from mid-sized, ≈13% from small) [reported/estimate, 424B4];
  - in Q1 2025, small businesses were 12% and mid-sized 79% [reported, 424B4, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1891027/000110465925052048/tm2413466-25_424b4.htm];
  - **97% of brands on MNTN are first-time TV advertisers** [reported, Q3'25 release];
  - clients are mostly e-commerce and travel SMBs, and many earn up to 40% of annual revenue in Q4 [third-party, AdExchanger 2026-02-12].

### 3.2 Mechanisms by which AI assistants could hurt MNTN

1. **Competition for the "new-channel test budget."**
   - CTV is the newest line item for 97% of MNTN's customers. ChatGPT ads are now the newest, most talked-about channel.
   - For an SMB with a fixed marketing budget (no committed contracts), the marginal experimental dollar is contested.
   - Likelihood: medium. Timing: 2026-2028.
2. **Attribution leakage.** MNTN sells "Performance TV" on verified visits and conversions. If more shopping journeys end inside agentic AI checkouts (ChatGPT shopping, Amazon Rufus, Meta's "Muse" agent; the latter's Sept 2026 launch was seen only in a snippet, unverified), last-touch credit could migrate to the assistant. That would make CTV's measured ROAS look worse even if its causal effect is unchanged.
   - Likelihood: medium-low by 2028. Impact: potentially larger than direct budget diversion, because MNTN's value proposition is measured ROAS.
3. **Self-serve parity.** MNTN's moat with SMBs is ease of use plus outcome measurement. OpenAI, Google, Meta and Amazon now all offer AI-automated, self-serve, outcome-bid buying. TV-specific creative (MNTN's QuickFrame AI) is the main differentiator left.

### 3.3 Mechanisms that could help MNTN

1. **Search cannibalization shrinks the budget AI ads draw from**, not CTV, per WPP and eMarketer. As the search share of budgets fragments, the relative weight of non-search channels can hold or rise.
2. **Declining organic and click-through traffic** from AI answers pushes SMBs to diversify paid demand creation. TV builds brand recall that drives branded and assistant queries. This is qualitative; there is no hard data.
3. **AI-generated video creative** lowers the biggest SMB barrier to TV (production cost). This is MNTN's "AI winner" leg (QuickFrame AI: 73,000+ signups YTD [reported, Q2'26 call]).

### 3.4 Quantifying the budget-migration drag on MNTN [estimate]

The logic: AI-assistant ads reach roughly **4% of total US ad spend by 2029** (eMarketer 13.6% of search × search ~29% of total; §2). That is about 1 percentage point of total spend per year from 2026 to 2029. MNTN's addressable wallet shrinks only by the share of that reallocation charged to CTV.

| Case | Assumption on source of AI-ad dollars | Cumulative CTV-wallet impact by 2029-30 | Annual MNTN growth drag 2027-30 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Base** | Mostly search and commerce (per WPP/eMarketer); CTV carries ≤ its pro-rata share (~6%) or less | ~0-4% | **~0 to −1 pp/yr** |
| **Bear** | SMBs treat AI ads and CTV as substitutable "new performance channels"; CTV test budgets cut disproportionately (2-3× pro-rata), plus attribution leakage | ~8-12% | **−2 to −3 pp/yr** |
| **Bull** | AI search erodes organic and search efficiency; SMBs diversify into video and TV; agentic shopping is attributed through MNTN's measurement integrations | Positive | **0 to +1 pp/yr** |

**Near-term (Q4 2026 to 2027):**

- ChatGPT's 2026 revenue (~$0.7B globally [estimate]) is ≈ 2.5% of US streaming-TV spend alone (0.7/27.6 [estimate]).
- MNTN has not cited AI-channel competition. Its customer count is still +40% [reported].
- Any drag is unmeasurable for now. **Watch for:** MNTN management commentary at Q3 (Nov 4); new-customer adds in TTM data; ARPU trends. TTM revenue per active customer fell ~9% y/y, from ~$81K to ~$74K [estimate: (70.0+87.1+73.7+82.5)/4,225 vs (57.1+69.8+64.5+68.5 − ~$14.8M Maximum Effort)/3,020], mostly from the shift toward smaller SMBs [reported driver, 10-K].

## 4. One-off items in y/y comparisons

### 4.1 2026 midterm political ad spend

- **Size:** AdImpact projects a record **$11.6B for the 2025-26 cycle**, versus $8.9B in 2022 and $11.2B in 2024 [third-party, AdImpact 2026-06-11, https://adimpact.com/blogs/adimpact-reveals-2026-election-cycle-to-reach-record-11.6-billion-in-ad-spending].
  - **CTV: $2.7B (raised from $2.5B), a 23% share**;
  - broadcast **$5.6B (48%)**;
  - digital $1.6B.
  - AdImpact's original Sept 2025 projection was $10.8B total and **$2.48B CTV** (versus $2.34B in 2024 and $1.09B in 2022) [third-party, The Current, snippet].
  - A blog citing AdImpact/Puck says broadcast plus digital had reached **$4.7B** by mid-October, excluding CTV and cable [third-party, Media Confidential 2026-10, low quality, snippet].
- **Timing:** Election Day is Nov 3, 2026. Vendors estimate ~50% of political dollars run in the final 30 days [third-party vendor blogs, unverified]. So the crowding sits in **late Q3 and October to early November (Q4)**.
  - Roku: Q2'26 political revenue already "exceeded the comparable quarter" of 2024, and spend "is expected to build through late Q3 and into Q4" [reported, Roku Q2'26 letter, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1428439/000162828026054321/a04fy2026q2exhibit991.htm].
- **Does MNTN run political ads? Yes, but immaterially, and it does not disclose political revenue.**
  - Risk factors refer to "the amount of political advertising spent through our platform" [reported, 424B4] and to political-ad regulation that "lacks clarity and uniformity" [reported, 10-K].
  - CEO Mark Douglas (Q2'26 call, 2026-08-04): MNTN "has historically not participated meaningfully" in political spending, which large agencies dominate. **Over 99% of MNTN inventory is bought via private-marketplace deals at pre-negotiated prices**, so open-market CPM spikes have limited effect [reported, paraphrase of transcript, https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/11/mntn-mntn-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/].
  - The same view was given in Feb 2026: no expected midterm bump because politics is about "blasting messages very broadly" [reported via AdExchanger 2026-02-12].
- **Precedent, Q4 2024 (presidential cycle with $2.34B of CTV political):**
  - MNTN Q4'24 revenue was ≈ **$69.8M versus ≈ $52.0M in Q4'23, about +34% y/y** [estimate].
  - Q4'24 is from XBRL: FY24 $225.571M minus 9M $155.759M. Q4'23 = 29.5% × $176.3M, from the 424B4 seasonality disclosure.
  - The crowding did not visibly dent growth. Q4'24 also included ~$5.6M of Maximum Effort revenue.
- **Q4 2026 guidance embeds it:**
  - FY26 guidance of $347-357M, minus 1H26 of $156.2M, minus the Q3 midpoint of $87.5M, implies **Q4'26 of ~$103-113M, or +19% to +30% y/y versus $87.1M** (midpoint ~$108M, +24%) [estimate from guidance].
  - Q3'26 guidance of $86-89M is **+23% to +27% y/y** versus $70.0M [estimate from guidance].
- **2027 comp:** there is no political revenue to lap, so MNTN's 2027 is not flattered or penalized the way Roku's or broadcasters' is. Lower open-market CPMs in Q4'27 could slightly improve customer ROAS.
- **Assessment:**
  - Likelihood that the midterms materially hurt Q4'26: **low (≤20%)**.
  - Impact if they do: **≤1-2% of Q4 revenue** [estimate], via ROAS-sensitive SMBs pausing in high-CPM DMAs.
  - 2027 comp effect: **neutral to slightly positive**.
  - OpenAI excludes political conversations from ads [third-party, launch coverage], so AI platforms do not compete for this money.

### 4.4 Tariffs (2025-26) and other one-offs

- The 10-K risk factors cite tariffs and retaliatory measures as sources of demand uncertainty [reported]. Tariffs were **not mentioned** in MNTN's Q2'25, Q3'25, Q1'26 or Q2'26 releases [reported, as read].
- Organic growth *accelerated* to +36% in Q4'25 [reported]. There is no evidence of a tariff-driven pullback in MNTN's numbers.
- At the SMB level, tariffs still bite:
  - an NFIB member quote in the Sept 2026 report says "Tariffs have had a major impact on our profit margins" [reported, NFIB SBET];
  - 24% of owners reporting lower profits cite rising material costs (+8 pts) [reported].
  - Many MNTN customers are e-commerce brands that import goods, so a margin squeeze is a latent risk to marketing budgets.
- **Other 2026 context, non-MNTN (relevant to CTV competition, not y/y comps):**
  - Fox agreed to acquire Roku on 2026-06-15 [reported, Roku Q2'26 letter].

  - MNTN added director Michael J. Katz, ex-T-Mobile, on 2026-09-03 [reported, 8-K]. Not a financial one-off.
- **Seasonality** (not a one-off but a comp driver): Q4 was 30.0% of FY25 revenue and Q1 22.2% (30.9% and 19.4% in FY24) [reported, 10-K]. Q4 holiday strength concentrates macro risk in one quarter.

### 5.4 MNTN downturn scenario math [estimate]

## 7. Thesis implications

- **AI-victim tilt from this topic: mild and back-end loaded.**
  - The AI-ads pool is forecast to approach the size of today's US streaming-TV market by 2029-30 (eMarketer $26B US; WPP >$100B global; OpenAI's own ~$100B).
  - OpenAI has copied the SMB self-serve, outcome-bid playbook, and SMBs adopted it at scale within months.
  - The best evidence (WPP, eMarketer, Google +17%, Meta +27%) says the money comes mainly from search and commerce budgets and from net market growth, not from CTV.
  - I model a **base drag of 0 to −1 pp/yr on 2027-2030 revenue growth**, with a bear case of −2 to −3 pp/yr, plus a tail risk to MNTN's measured ROAS from agentic checkout.
- **The bigger near-term swing factor is macro, not AI.**
  - MNTN's SMB-heavy, no-contract revenue base makes it high-beta.
  - A 2027 ad recession (my ~20-30% probability) could cut growth by 15-30+ pp for a year. That is far larger than any 2027 AI-diversion effect.
- **One-offs do not distort the 2027 setup.** There is no political revenue to lap, the Maximum Effort effect is finished, and the World Cup was not a disclosed revenue driver.
- The reacceleration implied by Q3 and Q4 guidance (+23-27% and ~+19-30%) is the near-term tell. Watch three things in the Nov 4 print:
  1. Q4 guidance versus the implied ~$108M midpoint;
  2. the TTM customer-growth trend (+40% at Q2);
  3. any management mention of ChatGPT or AI-search competition, or of political CPM effects.
