# MNTN (NYSE: MNTN): AI victim or AI winner? Outlook for 2027–2030

Research package as of **2026-10-15**, before Q3 2026 results (due Nov 4, 2026).

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`MNTN_AI_Deep_Dive_2026-10.md`](MNTN_AI_Deep_Dive_2026-10.md) | **The report.** Bottom line, business model, AI threat and upside tables, verdict and analogs, the 2026E–2030E model with three scenarios, bull/bear cases, pre-mortems, non-AI risks, quarterly scorecard and sources. |

| [`research/`](research/) | 15 research dossiers (A–O). Each was built from primary sources and ends with its adversarial verification section. |
| [`research/perspectives/`](research/perspectives/) | Independent agent outputs: bull and bear advocates, two pre-mortems, three verdict judges, the model red-team and the completeness critic. |

Number labels: **[R]** reported, **[R\*]** management statement on a call, **[G]** guidance, **[C]** consensus, **[3P]** third-party, **[E]** estimate. Not investment advice.
